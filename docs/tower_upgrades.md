# Tower upgrades

In Grimlock Game, left-click a constructed tower. The **Tower Info** card shows its
current level and combat statistics, followed by the next level's values, changes
and cost. Press **Upgrade** during Preparation. The card explains insufficient
gold or battle restrictions and shows **MAX LEVEL** after the final upgrade.
A brief gold range-ring highlight and confirmation provide upgrade feedback.
The ring immediately uses the upgraded range. Camera controls remain unchanged.

## Definitions

`game/assets/config/towers.json` owns all statistics. Each type has an ordered
`levels` array; its array positions are Levels 1, 2, 3, and so on. Both supplied
tower types have three explicitly authored levels:

| Tower | Level | Damage | Range | Shots/sec | Cost to enter level |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Small | 1 | 25 | 3.5 | 1.5 | 30 construction |
| Small | 2 | 45 | 4.2 | 2 | 25 upgrade |
| Small | 3 | 70 | 4.8 | 2.5 | 40 upgrade |
| Large | 1 | 60 | 5 | 2 | 50 construction |
| Large | 2 | 105 | 5.8 | 2.5 | 40 upgrade |
| Large | 3 | 160 | 6.5 | 3 | 65 upgrade |

For example, Small Tower's array is:

```json
"levels": [
  {"damage":25, "attack_range":3.5, "fire_rate":1.5, "upgrade_cost":0},
  {"damage":45, "attack_range":4.2, "fire_rate":2, "upgrade_cost":25},
  {"damage":70, "attack_range":4.8, "fire_rate":2.5, "upgrade_cost":40}
]
```

Every entry requires all four fields. Damage, range and shots/sec must be finite
and positive; shots/sec supports up to 60. Upgrade costs are nonnegative integers.
Level 1 has zero `upgrade_cost`; its purchase price remains the type's
`construction_cost`. No statistics or costs are generated by multipliers.
Adding an explicitly configured entry extends that type's maximum level without
changing combat or HUD code. Keep Large Tower stronger at equivalent levels when
editing the supplied catalog.

Projectile speed, size and color, models, visual scale and collision footprints
remain type-level properties. Existing models/materials are shared at every level;
upgrades require no new assets. Legacy flat catalogs still load as a single-level
type using their original values, with no invented upgrade levels.

## Runtime and persistence

`TowerLevelDefinition` contains immutable level statistics. `TowerProgress` holds
only a type and its current level, owned by TowerSystem and keyed by stable scene
object ID. Synchronization, transforms and temporary hiding retain progress;
deletion removes it. New towers start at Level 1.

`LevelSession::upgradeTower` checks the active level, Preparation phase and current
eligibility before delegating to TowerSystem. TowerSystem validates the target,
maximum level and affordability, then uses the existing EconomySystem transaction.
After a successful payment, incrementing the level cannot fail. Failed requests
never spend gold. Upgrades do not change scene transforms, footprints, identifiers
or NavMesh geometry.

Combat and the HUD read the same level definition. New projectiles copy that
level's damage; in-flight projectiles retain the damage they had when fired.
Targeting reads the new range and firing uses the new interval immediately.

Upgrade progress is runtime-only: it is never saved into scene or level JSON.
Restarting/reapplying a level resets every tower to Level 1, including authored
towers. Editor scene authoring remains unchanged; no initial-level field is needed.
Both applications continue using the shared GameLogic combat implementation.

## Checks

`Combat.Runtime` checks both types' upgraded targeting range, projectile and impact
damage, cadence, independent progress, in-flight damage preservation, stable
geometry/transforms, hidden-tower synchronization, max level, removal and reset.
`Game.Runtime` exercises real selection/Upgrade buttons, exact payments, insufficient
gold, maximum level, battle restrictions, combat/waves, restart and unchanged authored
files. `Economy.Cpu` checks the authored level definitions and rejects invalid or
incomplete statistics/costs. Run the README CMake presets and CTest commands for
both Game and Editor builds.
